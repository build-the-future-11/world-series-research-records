## Frozen decisions

- Closed project conclusions are not reopened by optional follow-up ideas.
- Protected/test results already exposed during prior work cannot be relabeled as fresh confirmation.
- Future successors require new versioning, new protocol/falsifier, new freeze, and a new protected evaluation boundary.
- Negative and mixed results remain in the record.
- Portal submission or external replication is not evidence missing from the current frozen study unless a venue or paper claim explicitly requires it.

## Current claims allowed at portfolio level

- The active current research backlog for the closed versions is zero.
- Multiple projects terminate in negative, mixed, or inconclusive outcomes.
- The World-Series workbench has a verified bounded release and reproducibility path.
- Some mechanisms or components show scoped effects, but broad superiority is not established.

## Claims not allowed

- All projects are positive.
- Every proposed architecture is novel.
- Synthetic benchmark gains imply external scientific discovery.
- Green CI alone establishes scientific efficacy.
- A blocked successor is a verified experiment.
- Optional successor work retroactively changes the conclusion of a frozen project.

## Unresolved items

These are not current-version research blockers:
- optional arXiv / venue submissions;
- independent replication;
- optional successor studies;
- broader data, natural-domain, or external-validation programs;
- any new claim that exceeds the current evidence.

## Protected evaluation boundary

No closed result may be tuned against after the fact and still be presented as confirmatory. Any new successor must define a fresh protected split or prospective evaluation before inspection and must record its own source commit, data version, metrics, seed policy, stopping rule, and exclusion policy.

## Completion criteria

The current versions are complete when:
1. the current claim has an explicit bounded endpoint;
2. frozen evidence and failures are retained;
3. unsupported stronger claims are withdrawn or qualified;
4. code/paper closeout exists where applicable; and
5. any future work is versioned as a successor rather than silently reopening the frozen study.

Those criteria are satisfied for the portfolio state recorded on 2026-09-30.
